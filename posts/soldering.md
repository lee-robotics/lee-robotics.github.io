## Factory pilot highlights
- **Throughput:** **108 motors** produced over **5 h**
- **Cycle time:** approx **160 s/motor** average (human takt time noted as approx **130 s**)
- **Reliability:** **99.4%** success per cable insertion.

## Why it matters
The result is a practical demonstration that **vision-based control** can sustain both **precision** and **uptime** on a real factory workflow—without turning the environment into a tightly scripted lab setup.
